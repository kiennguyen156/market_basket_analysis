# MARKET BASKET ANALYSIS
Welcome to my personal project!

This repository contains my mini project using Market Basket Analysis framework for reporting.
## 🛒 **POTENTIAL COMBOS AND CUSTOMER PSYCHOLOGY**
An analysis uncovering customer shopping behaviors and frequent product combinations to optimize revenue through targeted cross-selling and store layout strategies.

* **Algorithmic Pattern Discovery:** Implemented the Apriori algorithm and self-join techniques across 12,316 sales transactions to identify high-probability purchase combinations, utilizing a 0.2% minimum support and 20% confidence threshold to eliminate noise while capturing profitable niche cross-selling opportunities.
* **Behavioral Psychology Insights:** Decoded consumer intent, revealing a "beauty from within" trend where shampoo and conditioner buyers frequently cross-purchased vitamins and supplements (Lift score > 5.0). Additionally, identified a strong "At-Home Spa" and self-care segment driving the combined sales of candles, diffusers, bath oils, and face masks (Lift > 1.75).
* **Actionable Cross-Selling Strategy:** Proposed data-driven business interventions including targeted "Frequently Bought Together" UI pop-ups, themed bundles (e.g., "Weekend Spa Kit" and "Hair Savior Bundle"), and automated 48-hour marketing workflows via Email/Zalo to maximize customer lifetime value.

## 🔧 Tech Stack
* **Pandas –** Data ingestion, transformation, and pre-processing.
* **Mlxtend –** Transaction encoding, Apriori algorithm execution, and association rule mining.
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn –** Exploratory data analysis, outlier detection, and visualization of subcategory combination.
